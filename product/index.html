A sensor reading matters when it helps someone make a better call.

Veldren Console connects the information around that decision: which asset is involved, what changed in its signal, who has reviewed the finding, and what maintenance action followed. The context travels with the work.

That gives operators, reliability teams, and plant leaders a common place to understand what needs attention today—and what the plant has learned over time.

A considered technology path

Powerful under the hood.
Clear on the floor.

The product experience stays focused on assets, decisions, and maintenance. The NVIDIA stack is the supporting path for bringing more signal processing and model capacity to the places Veldren runs.

These are selected building blocks for the planned AI and edge architecture; rollout will follow product validation and deployment needs.

JetPack SDK On-site edge

For local processing on NVIDIA Jetson modules near production equipment, where timely analysis matters.

RAPIDS · cuDF · cuML Data and learning

GPU-accelerated data preparation and machine learning for equipment baselines and anomaly scoring.

NeMo Framework Maintenance language

A path to specialized language models grounded in manuals, procedures, and repair histories—with people reviewing the result.

Triton Inference Server Model delivery

For serving multiple production models consistently across facilities as deployments grow.

CUDA Toolkit Signal processing

Low-level acceleration for signal analysis such as transforms and pattern calculations on high-frequency data.
